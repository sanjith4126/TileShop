Our Heritage

The Bhavani
Legacy

Founded in 2014, Suwasthick Tiles was born from a singular vision — to make quality tiles and sanitaryware accessible without compromising on quality or tradition. Located in the heart of Bhavani, opposite the historic Kalyana Mandabam, our showroom has become the go-to destination for architects, interior designers, and homeowners across Tamil Nadu.

We specialise exclusively in premium ceramic tiles and sanitaryware — a focused craft that lets us perfect every detail. Each piece is backed by our 10-year quality warranty, so your investment is protected for years to come.

With over 40,000 successful orders across residential, commercial, and restoration projects, we have built not just beautiful floors and walls, but lasting relationships. Our clients span engineers, architects, masons, and everyday homeowners who trust us to make their spaces extraordinary.
